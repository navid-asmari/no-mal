Zwei Spure
by Stubete Gäng

Two tracks lead through the snow
There at the edge of the path you can see them
Look at the tracks, they lead together
Nowhere else

Two tracks lead through the forest
In the dark you only half see them
At the end the tracks lead all the same
Back to the light

Two tracks lead side by side
Sometimes very close, sometimes far apart
Sometimes deep and heavy, then lighter again
Always further

Two tracks lead through the snow
Where you can almost see the spring already
And they still run back there
Until they disappear with the snow
